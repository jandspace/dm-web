Three loaders, one per job: the Spinning Wheel while a page loads, the Ellipsis where content is still coming, and the Three-Quarters Circle on an action (Button, Input). Figma: Web UI Kit › Loading (214:6737). Doc: docs/4_Components__04_Loading.md.

Page loading — Spinning Wheel

A 60% white cover over the whole page with the wheel in the middle. The page under it can't be clicked or scrolled. The wheel turns in 12 steps, one bar at a time. Below it is kept inside a box; the button shows it over this page for 3 seconds.
