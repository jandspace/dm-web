Media

An image or video filling a fixed-ratio box: .media plus one ratio modifier, with the img or video set to object-fit: cover. The box takes its width from the parent; the card or module sets the corner radius. Figma: Web UI Kit › Media (108:3533). Doc: docs/4_Components__05_Media.md.

Image crops

Editors crop each image in the CMS (Article › Images). Every crop has its own field, so one photo can be framed differently per size. The 302 Preview and the vertical 2:3 image are set in another part of the CMS, not in the article page.

Class CMS crop Feed size Ratio Used in
.media--banner Banner Preview (optional) 964 × 544 ≈ 16:9 Article Card Banner, MD and up
.media--portrait-banner Mobile portrait banner (optional) 750 × 700 15:14 Article Card Banner, SM-XS
.media--large Large Preview 308 × 185 358:215 (≈ 5:3) Article Card XL (816 × 490 at XL), also MD
.media--puff Puff Image 154 × 115 4:3 Article Card SM thumbs (Left / Right Thumb)
.media--small Small Preview 87 × 84 ≈ 1:1 Other areas, not Article Card (to be confirmed)
.media--302 302 Preview (another CMS part) 302 × 322 15:16 Article Card Image Only
.media--vertical Vertical (another CMS part) 400 × 600 2:3 Article Card MD (Vertical Media)

Video

Footage is 16:9 by default (.media--video); the column sets the width. When an Article Card varies its media area, the video takes the card's ratio instead: --vertical (2:3, MD Vertical Media) or --302 (15:16, Image Only Medium), cropped to fill. Controls come from Video Player Controls (modules/video-player-controls/). Vertical footage such as Shorts uses --shorts (9:16) — video only, never a photo.
