Modal

Focused content or a required action above the page, over a scrim. Elevation 5. Three sizes — Default, Large and Fullwidth (a bottom sheet) — across SM-XS, MD and LG. Built on the native <dialog>; the concept follows Bootstrap's modal. Figma: Web UI Kit › Modal (3358:62). Doc: docs/4_Components__07_Modal.md.

Try it

Each Button opens the modal below. Close it with the close button, a footer button, Esc or a click on the scrim. Resize the window to see the breakpoints: from MD the modal sits at the top, on SM-XS in the middle.

Viewports

The modal open in a page at each breakpoint — size, position and the Fullwidth height cap (50% from MD, 80% on SM-XS) follow the preview, not the window. Use the buttons inside the preview to open it again.

Viewport

Anatomy

The box open on a scrim. Header (title + close, keyline below), body, footer (keyline above, buttons on the right). Without a title there is no header: the close button stays in the corner over the body, and the body starts at the top. The footer is optional.
