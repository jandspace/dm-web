Toggle

A switch that commits at once — on or off, no confirm step. Blue (interaction-primary) by default; green (interaction-positive) only where a Positive button would be right. Figma: Web UI Kit › Toggle Switch (4277:82221). Doc: docs/4_Components__10_Toggle.md.

Try it

Click a switch or its label, or Tab to one and press Space. Several toggles on one screen are all blue.

Positive

Green only when the action is as affirmative as an Approve or Activate button — rare, and never next to blue toggles.

States

Hover is Web only (shown here with .is-hover). Disabled sets the whole switch to 25%, colours unchanged.
