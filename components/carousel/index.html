Carousel

Items in a row that scrolls sideways and snaps an item to the start. Two types, by how they show there is more: Row — several items in view, the one cut off on the right (with a 16px fade) shows it, no dots; Slide — one item fills the box, dots show it. Arrows at 40% until hovered: a Slide has them at every width (on a phone they and the dots say "this moves"); a Row from LG only (below LG the cut-off item and swipe are enough). Row — cut off on the right, no dots

--carousel-gap 8 · --carousel-inset 16 · --carousel-end 16. Next brings the item cut off on the right to the start; Prev the reverse.

Row — With Auto-Play

The same Row with data-autoplay: every 5s it moves as Next does; after the end, back to the first. No dots, so nothing shows the wait. Waits while hovered, focused or off screen; none with reduced motion.

Slide — Static

One item fills the box. Dots inside the picture, over a scrim; arrows at its vertical centre. Theme Pinned (data-theme="light"). Moves only by arrow or swipe.

Slide — With Auto-Play

The same Slide with data-autoplay: the active dot fills in 5s, then the next slide; after the last, the first. Waits while hovered, focused or off screen; none with reduced motion.
