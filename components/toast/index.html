Toast

Short feedback that floats over everything — modals included — and goes. Five contexts: Success, Error, Warning, Information, Neutral. Top centre by default (bottom centre when needed); new toasts stack in front. Theme-pinned to light. Figma: Web UI Kit › Toast (3265:1886). Doc: docs/4_Components__09_Toast.md.

Try it

Each button shows a sample toast. Toasts without buttons go after 3 seconds (paused while the pointer or focus is on them); toasts with buttons and errors stay until closed. Show a few in a row to see the stack: the older ones step back 16px and shrink a little — hover to spread them out.
