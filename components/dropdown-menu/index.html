Dropdown Menu

The panel that drops down from a trigger: an optional title, then a list of items to pick from or act on. Elevation 4 — it closes on an outside click. Click an item to select it. Figma: Web UI Kit › Dropdowns (175:5715). Doc: docs/4_Components__03_Dropdown-Menu.md.

Try it

A Button opens the menu below it. Pick an item to change the button label; an outside click or Esc closes it. The Dropdown Title is optional: Sort by goes without it (a Group Title heads the list), Your team uses it. Your team is capped at 333px, so its list scrolls under the title.
