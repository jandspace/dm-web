Tooltip & Popover

Two bubbles on one shape, as in Bootstrap. Popover — rich (icon, title, description, buttons, close), opened by a click. Tooltip — a short label on hover or keyboard focus, or flashed after an action. Figma: Web UI Kit › Tooltip (Popover) (3341:6789). Doc: docs/4_Components__11_Tooltip.md.

Try it — Popover

Click a button. Outside click or Esc closes it. Near an edge the bubble stays 16px inside the window and the tail moves to keep pointing at the button; without room on its side it flips.
