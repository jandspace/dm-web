Classes

.container
Page width: fluid below LG (max 720 · 960), fixed 996 at LG and 1224 at XL. No padding.
.layout-full
Figma “Fullwidth”. One section across the container: a single module, a split or a row, with an optional section header on top. Keyline below.
.layout-split
Puts Alpha and Beta side by side from LG (2/3 + 1/3). Below LG they stack, Beta under Alpha.
.layout-split--joined
One channel block split 2:1 (e.g. Showbiz): no keyline between Alpha and Beta.
.layout-alpha
Figma “Alpha”. The main column: 664 at LG, 816 at XL. Holds cards or rows.
.layout-beta
Figma “Beta”. The right rail: 332 at LG (the narrowest that fits an MPU), 408 at XL. No padding of its own. Keyline on its left.
.layout-row
Figma “Row”. Columns of equal width from MD — two children make Col 1-2, three Col 1-3, four 1/4. Below MD (SM-XS) the columns stack, with a keyline between them. Keyline below.
.layout-col
Figma “Col 1-n”. One column; its cards stack and it keeps its own height. Put cards straight in — no wrapper per card.
.layout-inset
Wraps a box with its own background (e.g. a tinted box in Beta) so it does not run edge to edge. One content block, like a card: 16px inset and a keyline below.

Rules

  • No gaps or padding on layout classes — every card carries its own 16px inset, so neighbours sit 32px apart.
  • Keylines: 1px translucent-dark/pale, 16px in from each end. Sections, rows and cards draw their own; the last child of its parent has none.
  • SM cards: Left Thumb in Alpha and other main columns, Right Thumb in Beta.
  • Hiding or reordering a block at a breakpoint is a page decision, not a layout class.
