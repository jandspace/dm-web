Elevation

Drop shadows that lift a surface above the page — a visual cue that it sits higher in priority or hierarchy. Five levels, var(--elevation-1) … var(--elevation-5) in styles/scss/foundation/_elevation.scss, from the Figma effect styles Elevation Lv-1…5 (Web UI Kit › Elevation, page 63:9380). Level 0 is flat. Each level is a role — pick the role first, and the role gives the shadow.

Which level?

Ask one question: how far does it sit from the page, and how much does it stop the reader? The further it floats and the more it interrupts, the higher the level. Never pick a shadow by eye — pick the role, and the role gives the level.

Level Role The test DM examples
0 · none Page Scrolls with the page. Content is split by keylines, not shadows. Page, Article Card, sections, Beta boxes
1 · --elevation-1 Sticky Stuck to an edge of the page; content scrolls underneath. Sticky Navbar
2 · --elevation-2 Floating control A small control resting on top of content. Carousel arrows, back-to-top button
3 · --elevation-3 Floating panel A panel that floats over the page and stays while the reader carries on. Mini video player
4 · --elevation-4 Temporary Appears for a moment — opened by the reader or by the system — and closes on an outside click or by itself. Dropdown, Nav Panel, Tooltip, Toast
5 · --elevation-5 Modal Blocks the page until it is dealt with; always over a scrim. The top layer. Modal
