Navigation — Masthead

Topbar · Branding · Navbar L1 (Figma Web UI Kit › Masthead 71:2568), plus the Mweb Nav Sheet (332:8626). The masthead always fills the browser width; its content sits in the page container. Below 768 the masthead takes the channel colour and the burger opens the Nav Sheet. On LG+ with a mouse, channels with subchannels (every channel except Crime Desk) open a dropdown panel on hover, with a short hover-intent delay (open 150ms · close 300ms). Links are placeholders (#). On scroll: MD+ shows the Sticky Navbar once the masthead has gone (Sticky menus pick its right side); Mweb keeps the nav row and hides the logo block until you scroll up — scroll inside a viewport preview to try it.
