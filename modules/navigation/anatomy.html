Navigation — Anatomy

Every part of the Masthead on its own, with its class. Desktop parts (≥768) are shown on this page; Mweb parts (<768) in a 390px frame below, so their real Mweb styles apply. Hover and open states are shown with the static helpers .is-hover / .is-open.

Desktop

1. Topbar

Desktop only (≥768). 32px row with a light keyline. Channel id (3px channel-colour bar) and Last updated (LG+) on the left; Mail+ Subscribe · Edition · Login · Search on the right, each with a 1px keyline on its left. Subscribe sits here on channel pages; on Home and Daily Mail+ home (.masthead--landing) it moves to the Branding slot.

2. Branding

Logo on the left (LG+ 331×68 · MD 267×55); the slot on the right takes the Mail+ Subscribe button by default, or another branding element (max 480px).

3. Navbar L1 item

Desktop: 38px, label-sm Bold. The 4px indicator takes the item’s channel colour (.masthead-nav__item--{section}): shown on the current channel and slid up on hover. Items with subchannels (LG+, mouse) skip the bar: hover only darkens the label, then the item turns into a subtlest tab and opens the dropdown panel.

Mweb

4. Mweb masthead

Below 768 the masthead takes the channel colour. White logo, Subscribe pill (primary on dark), round login (secondary on dark), burger and white labels; the current channel gets a 4px white pill.

5. Nav Sheet

Opened by the burger. Full-screen, lightest.
